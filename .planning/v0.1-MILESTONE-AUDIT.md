## Critical Blockers

### B1 — Phase 9 evidence no longer matches the current verifier

`scripts/phase-09-mutation-battery.mjs` now requires release-authorization data including `mode`, `releaseAuthorization`, `runAttempt`, `sharedVersion`, `consumedChangesets`, and `versionReceipt`. The checked-in `09-MUTATION-EVIDENCE.json` and `09-RELEASE-EVIDENCE.json` predate that schema; release evidence also lacks current `consumerTooling` data.

Reproduced by the integration checker:

```text
$ pnpm run check:phase09:evidence
FAIL: mutation evidence mode must be feature or versioned
```

This exact check is transitively used by `check:phase09`, `check:phase09:release`, GitHub CI, and the release workflow before archive export. CI and release cannot complete.

**Affected requirements:** PKG-04, ADP-01, ADP-02, ADP-03, ADP-04.

### B2 — Phase 9 is unverified

Phases 1–8 have `*-VERIFICATION.md`; Phase 9 has validation, mutation, release, and security evidence but no `09-VERIFICATION.md`. ADP-01 through ADP-04 are therefore absent from every phase verifier. Orphan detection overrides their checked boxes and summary claims: all four are **unsatisfied until independently verified**.

### B3 — Requirement evidence is incomplete

Nine requirements have satisfying verifier evidence and checked ledger rows, but no `requirements-completed` entry in any SUMMARY frontmatter: CAT-02, CAT-05, CAT-06, CAT-07, SEC-01, SEC-05, PKG-02, PKG-03, and DX-03. They are **partial** under the required three-source matrix.

SEC-03 is also **partial**: its Phase 4 verifier records a consumer-supplied `jsonSchema` getter carve-out. The current integration audit found later source and tests that appear to close this channel, but it cannot be promoted until Phase 4 is re-verified or a later verifier explicitly owns the closure.
