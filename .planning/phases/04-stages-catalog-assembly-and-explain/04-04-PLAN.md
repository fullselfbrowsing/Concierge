<objective>
Prove CAT-03 against the built artifact: the typo that fails, the self-reference that fails under its
own code, the forward reference that must NOT fail, and the aggregation that survives a post-pass
appending after every per-action issue. Plus the type-level pin that stops `CatalogIssueCode` being
widened.

Purpose: three of these four cases exist because the *obvious* implementation gets them wrong. An
in-loop check reports a forward reference as missing (false positive), misses self-reference
entirely because `seenNames.add(action.name)` has already run, and — since Phase 4 appends
cross-stage actions last — would fail every build whose consent policy points at a cross-stage
action. The forward-reference case is the one that can only go green on a post-pass, and mutant
M-04-11 is the only mutant that proves the check reads the *complete* name set rather than a prefix.

Output: four new cases in `test/catalog.test.ts`'s C-series and one union pin in
`test-d/catalog.test-d.ts`.
</objective>
