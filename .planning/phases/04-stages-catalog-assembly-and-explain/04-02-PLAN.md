<objective>
Land CAT-03 inside `buildCatalog`: two new `CatalogIssueCode` members, a post-pass over the complete
declared-name set placed between the existing per-action loop and the existing single throw, and an
`export` on `deepFreeze` so `explain()` does not have to grow a second, weaker freeze walk.

Purpose: CAT-03 is the check that catches a typo which would otherwise silently disable a safety
gate. Its placement is the whole plan, and it was measured rather than argued: with the check inside
the existing loop, a `requires` naming *any* action declared later is reported missing — and because
Phase 4 feeds `buildCatalog` `[...allStageActions, ...crossStage]` with cross-stage appended last,
`{consent: {requires: "signOut"}}` would fail every build. In-loop also misses self-reference
entirely, because `seenNames.add(action.name)` has already run by the time the check fires.

Output: `src/catalog.ts` with the post-pass, the two codes, the exported `deepFreeze`, and three
pieces of prose that this phase makes false or incomplete corrected in place.
</objective>
