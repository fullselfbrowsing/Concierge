## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-23 | Tampering | `ConciergeConfig.digest` supplied by the app | accept | The app is already fully trusted — the catalog author is the app author. | T-01-24 | Spoofing | a seam being silently widened so an unrelated function satisfies it | mitigate | `_configPresentReadback`, `_configDigest`, `_configScheduler` are `Equals` assertions, not `Assignable` — a widening breaks them. Verified defect-first in this plan. |
