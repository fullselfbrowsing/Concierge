<objective>
Close two transport-side defects, both of which are genuine post-publish breaking changes because
`ToolBatch` and `TransportCapabilities` are interfaces consumers **implement** (D-09).

1. `ToolBatch.deferUntilDelivered` still carries the pre-fix `(effect: (deliveredResponseId: string) => void) => void`.
   `InvocationMeta`'s twin was fixed; the transport-side hook — the one Success Criterion 1 is
   actually about — was not. A consumer that cannot see `outcome` cannot refuse a truncated readback.
2. `TransportCapabilities.userTurnIdentity: boolean` records *whether* turn identity exists but not
   *where it comes from*. On a voice transport the microphone picks up the assistant's own TTS and
   the recognizer transcribes it as user speech — **the agent's own output mints a new user turn**,
   which is exactly what `bindTo: "userTurn"` accepts as proof that a human acted. A boolean cannot
   express the difference between a button press and a recognizer.

Purpose: TRN-05 is the one item in this phase that unambiguously could not wait. TRN-01 is
demonstrated at type level here because Phase 7 owns the stub transport.
Output: `TurnIdentityProvenance`, the two corrected fields, and `transport.test-d.ts`.

**Serial phase.** Plan 3 of 9 in a single serial sequence. Do not parallelize.
</objective>
