Close WR-06 and IN-03. **WR-06 is a decision, not a fix — do not make it silently.**

`ActionResult`'s `ok: boolean` and `reason?: ReasonCode | undefined` are independent, so
`{ ok: true, reason: "handler_error", message: "Done." }` typechecks and so does
`{ ok: false, message: "Failed." }` — a failure carrying no code. `ReasonCode`'s doc (`:168-179`) is
emphatic that the closed union exists to preserve "the exhaustiveness every dispatcher and consent
mapper depends on". A success carrying a failure code runs that exhaustive mapper on a result that
did not fail; a failure carrying no code is the state the closed union was supposed to make
impossible to under-report. Both are handed to the model via `Transport.respond` (`:1019`).

Reshaping `ActionResult` after publish is breaking, which is what puts it inside this phase's stated
scope. The reviewer graded it warning-not-blocker because the flat shape may have been chosen
deliberately and they "found no record of that trade in `01-CONTEXT.md`, `01-VALIDATION.md`, or the
file's own comments".

**The record exists. It is in `01-CONTEXT.md` § D-01 under "Rejected — do not revive", and it is not
the rationale the reviewer hypothesised.** D-01 rejects the discriminated union not for
`Transport.respond` serialization but because *"`Pick<ActionResult, "reason"|"message">` errors
TS2344 (`keyof` a union is the key intersection), and every `.reason` read needs an `ok === false`
guard, landing friction in the dispatcher and kernel."* The reviewer searched for a serialization
