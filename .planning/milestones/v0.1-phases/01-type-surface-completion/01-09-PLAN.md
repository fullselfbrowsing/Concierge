<objective>
The phase gate. A green suite is not evidence of a working suite.

This is not a formality. A ten-mutant battery was run during research against a *first-draft* suite
built by someone who had just read all seven success criteria — and **three mutants escaped**: the
`ReadbackSink` form (M5), the `ToolBatch` delivery hook (M2), and `ActionDefinition.handler` dropping
its type arguments (M8). Each needed a purpose-built assertion. A suite that has never been seen to
fail is a suite that might be green over a broken contract.

Plans 02 through 07 each verified their own mutants defect-first as they landed. This plan re-runs
all ten against the *final* state of `types.ts`, because an assertion that fired in isolation can
stop firing after a later edit changes an adjacent declaration.

Purpose: Success Criterion 7 says the suite must fail when any corrected defect is reintroduced.
This plan is the only thing that establishes that as fact rather than intent.
Output: the battery record, the three remaining phase-gate checks, and the validation sign-off.

**Serial phase.** Plan 9 of 9 in a single serial sequence. Do not parallelize.

`packages/concierge/src/types.ts` is listed in `files_modified` because the battery edits it
transiently. **Every mutant must be reverted.** The plan leaves the file byte-identical to how
Plan 08 left it.

## ⚠ Judge each mutant against ITS OWN guard, not a generic one

Two rows in earlier drafts named guards that cannot fire for their mutant. Both were corrected on
2026-07-28 after the checker compiled the mutations:

- **M4** does not produce TS2578. No `@ts-expect-error` directive anywhere in the suite becomes
  unused when `ConsentAck` flattens. Its guards are `_attestedNeedsHash` (TS2344) and the narrowing
  function (TS2322).
- **M10** does not fire `_requiresIsString`. `ConsentPolicy<Booking>["requires"]` is still `string`
  once the mutation's `Name` parameter carries its `= string` default. Its guards are
  `_nameNotWidened` and `_snapshotInferred`, both TS2344.

`_requiresIsString` remains a good static pin against `requires` being retyped outright — it is
simply not M10's detector. **Do not "fix" a working suite to make a silent pin speak.** If a mutant
exits non-zero and names the guards listed in its row, that mutant passes.
</objective>
